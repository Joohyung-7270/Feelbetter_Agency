# FeelBetter — Brand film brief (reference)

This is the creative brief for the 50–60 second FeelBetter.agency brand film. The storefront theme in this repository borrows its copy, color and tone from this brief, so it is kept here as the single source of truth for brand voice.

## The one story

> "I saw something I wanted. I had no idea where to buy it. FeelBetter found it for me."

Not a corporate ad, not a generic e-commerce commercial. One simple, relatable story.

## Style

- Premium lifestyle film. Natural, youthful, contemporary Seoul.
- Warm cinematic lighting. Editorial fashion-film aesthetic.
- Subtle handheld camera movement mixed with smooth tracking shots. Authentic human behavior.
- Minimal UI overlays. Fast but elegant pacing.
- No exaggerated futuristic AI graphics. The technology should feel invisible, simple and human.

## Color

Deep navy, warm ivory, natural sunlight and subtle green accents inspired by FeelBetter branding.

| Navy | `#0B1F3A` | Text on ivory, primary buttons, dark sections |
| Deep navy | `#06152B` | Footer / darkest sections |

## Main character

A stylish young adult in Seoul. Gender-neutral styling. Casual contemporary streetwear. Natural rather than influencer-like.

## Story

### Scene 1 — Discovery (0–7 s)

Late afternoon in Seoul. The protagonist walks through a trendy neighborhood and suddenly notices someone riding an unusually beautiful skateboard. Close-ups: wheels rolling across pavement, deck graphics, shoes pushing against the street, sunlight hitting the skateboard. The protagonist turns and looks at it. The skateboard passes by. They instinctively take out their phone.

Internal feeling: "Wait… that's nice."

On-screen text: **"Ever seen something you wanted…"** — cut — **"…but had no idea where to buy it?"**

### Scene 2 — The search problem (7–15 s)

The protagonist sits at a café and tries searching for the skateboard. Several browser tabs. Different shops. Different countries. Out of stock. Wrong model. Unclear shipping. Different prices. Not stressful or comedic — simply the friction of finding one specific product online. The protagonist pauses, then remembers FeelBetter. Phone screen: **feelbetter.agency**

### Scene 3 — Just ask (15–23 s)

The FeelBetter.agency request interface. The protagonist casually types:

> "I saw this skateboard in Seoul. Black deck, minimal graphic. Something like this. Can you find it?"

They optionally attach the photo they took. No complicated forms. No account creation. No endless filters. The request is submitted.

On-screen typography: **"Say what you want."** then **"Words. A link. A photo."**

### Scene 4 — AI × Human (23–32 s)

The phone transitions into a designed visual sequence of the FeelBetter process. The message becomes a structured sourcing brief:

```
ITEM       Skateboard
STYLE      Black / Minimal
REFERENCE  Uploaded photo
```

Then the human side: a FeelBetter concierge at a laptop checks several sources — product model, seller, stock, price, shipping availability. Avoid portraying AI as replacing people.

On-screen text: **"AI finds the signal."** **"Human concierge checks the details."**

### Scene 5 — Found it (32–40 s)

Phone notification. Email from **Feelbetter Concierge**: **"Your link is ready."** The protagonist opens it. One clean product card:

```
MATCH FOUND
Verified seller · In stock · Ready to buy
[ BUY NOW ]
```

The protagonist smiles slightly. Not exaggerated. Just the small satisfaction of "That's exactly it."

### Scene 6 — From want to reality (40–49 s)

Cinematic match cut: the product image on the phone becomes the real skateboard arriving. Package opens. Hands lift the skateboard. Close-ups of the exact details seen at the beginning — deck, wheels, texture. Then the protagonist rides the skateboard through Seoul, camera tracking alongside, golden-hour sunlight. The original moment of curiosity has become a real experience.

### Scene 7 — Brand ending (49–60 s)

The skateboard rolls past camera. Cut to a clean warm ivory background. FeelBetter logo.

> **See it.**
> **Want it.**
> **We'll find it.**

Pause. Then:

> Discover Better.
> Experience Better.
> Feel Better.
>
> FeelBetter.agency

Final micro-copy: **"One message. One hour. One link."** End on the FeelBetter logo.

## Emotional arc

Curiosity → Friction → Simplicity → Trust → Satisfaction

## Core message

FeelBetter is not another marketplace. You do not browse thousands of products. You simply tell FeelBetter what you want.

- AI understands the request.
- A human concierge verifies the options.
- FeelBetter sends you a ready-to-buy link.

The experience should feel like asking a knowledgeable friend: "Hey, where can I buy this?" — and getting the answer.
